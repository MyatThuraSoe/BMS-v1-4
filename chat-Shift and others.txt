
# Product Requirements Document  
## Cash Shift Modification: Cash Drawer Shift vs Non-Cash Employee Shift

**Project:** LumiPOS / BMS-v1-4  
**Scope:** Offline/LAN shop POS operations  
**Feature Area:** Cash Shift, Employee Shift, Payment Handling, Drawer Reconciliation, Reports, POS UX  
**Important Exclusion:** Do **not** implement money rounding / cash rounding. This feature is not required.

---

# 1. Problem Statement

Currently, the system treats a cashier shift mainly as a **cash drawer shift**.

That creates a real-world problem:

- Some employees only sell using mobile wallet, bank transfer, card, or credit.
- They do not handle cash.
- They do not need access to the cash drawer.
- The shop may have only **one physical cash drawer**.
- Forcing every employee to open a cash shift creates fake drawer reconciliation, confusing variance reports, and unnecessary friction.

Example:

| Employee | Activity | Needs Cash Drawer? |
|---|---|---|
| Aung | Main cashier, accepts cash | Yes |
| Thida | Sells using KBZPay / WavePay only | No |
| Kyaw | Floor sales using tablet, wallet only | No |

The system must allow **multiple employees to sell at the same time**, while only the responsible cashier/manage user controls the physical cash drawer.

---

# 2. Core Requirement

Separate the concept of:

## A. Employee Work Shift

Tracks what an employee did during their working period.

Examples:

- Sales made by Thida
- Wallet payments accepted by Thida
- Refunds processed by Thida
- Discounts given by Thida
- Voids performed by Thida

An employee work shift may or may not involve cash.

## B. Cash Drawer Session

Tracks the physical cash drawer.

Examples:

- Opening float
- Cash sales
- Cash refunds
- Safe drops
- Pay-ins / pay-outs
- Closing cash count
- Cash over/short variance

Only employees who are authorized to handle cash should be attached to a cash drawer session.

---

# 3. Main Business Rules

## 3.1 All sales must belong to an employee shift

Every completed sale must record:

```text
employeeShiftId
userId
saleDate
paymentMethods
```

This gives employee accountability even when no cash is involved.

---

## 3.2 Only cash payments require a cash drawer session

A sale can be completed without a cash drawer session **only if all payment methods are non-cash**.

Allowed without drawer:

- Mobile wallet
- Card
- Bank transfer
- Credit sale
- Store credit
- Loyalty redemption
- Other non-cash payment methods

Not allowed without drawer:

- Cash payment
- Cash refund
- Cash payout
- Cash tendering
- Any transaction that physically touches the drawer

---

## 3.3 Only one active cash drawer session per physical drawer

If the shop has one drawer, only one drawer session can be open at a time.

Example:

```text
Main Drawer
Status: OPEN
Opened by: Aung
Opening float: 100,000 MMK
```

Another employee cannot open a second independent cash drawer session for the same drawer.

They must either:

1. Open a **Non-Cash Shift**, or
2. Join the existing drawer session if shared drawer mode is enabled and permission is granted.

---

## 3.4 Non-cash employees must not see cash reconciliation

A wallet-only employee should not be asked:

- Opening float
- Expected cash
- Actual cash counted
- Drawer variance
- Safe drop
- Cash drawer close

Their close-shift screen should only show:

- Total sales
- Payment method summary
- Refunds
- Discounts
- Voids
- Number of transactions

---

# 4. Shift Types

Add the following shift types:

```java
public enum ShiftType {
    CASH_DRAWER,
    NON_CASH,
    SUPERVISOR
}
```

## 4.1 `CASH_DRAWER`

For employees who handle cash.

Can:

- Accept cash payments
- Process cash refunds if permitted
- Perform drawer movements
- Be attached to an active drawer session

Must:

- Be linked to a drawer session
- Follow cash drawer reconciliation rules

---

## 4.2 `NON_CASH`

For employees who do not touch cash.

Can:

- Sell using mobile wallet
- Sell using card
- Sell using bank transfer
- Create credit sales if allowed
- Process non-cash refunds if allowed

Cannot:

- Accept cash
- Give cash refund
- Open cash drawer
- Perform safe drop
- Close cash drawer
- See expected cash amount

Does not require:

- Opening float
- Cash count
- Cash variance

---

## 4.3 `SUPERVISOR`

For manager/owner override.

Can:

- Override blocked operations
- Approve cash refund
- Approve manual discount
- Join or close drawer sessions
- Force-close shift with reason

Important: Supervisor override must still be audited.

A supervisor must not be able to secretly accept cash outside drawer reconciliation.  
If a supervisor accepts cash, the transaction must still be linked to a valid drawer session.

---

# 5. Recommended Data Model

## 5.1 Reuse Existing `CashShift` as Employee Shift

To avoid breaking the existing system too much, keep the existing `CashShift` table/entity but expand its meaning.

It becomes the employee work shift.

Add fields:

```java
@Enumerated(EnumType.STRING)
@Column(name = "shift_type", nullable = false)
private ShiftType shiftType = ShiftType.CASH_DRAWER;

@ManyToOne(fetch = FetchType.LAZY)
@JoinColumn(name = "drawer_session_id")
private CashDrawerSession drawerSession;
```

Existing fields such as:

- openedBy
- closedBy
- openedAt
- closedAt
- status
- openingFloat
- expectedCash
- countedCash
- variance

can remain for backward compatibility, but for new logic:

- Drawer cash values should come from `CashDrawerSession`
- Non-cash shifts should use zero/null cash values
- Employee performance should come from sales linked to the shift

---

## 5.2 New Entity: `CashDrawer`

Represents a physical drawer.

```java
public class CashDrawer {
    private Long id;

    private String name; 
    // Example: "Main Drawer"

    private String code;
    // Example: "DRAWER-001"

    private Boolean isActive;

    private LocalDateTime createdAt;
    private LocalDateTime deletedAt;
}
```

For the first version, seed one default drawer:

```text
id: 1
name: Main Drawer
code: MAIN
```

---

## 5.3 New Entity: `CashDrawerSession`

Represents one open/close cycle of the physical drawer.

```java
public class CashDrawerSession {
    private Long id;

    private CashDrawer drawer;

    private User openedBy;
    private User closedBy;

    private LocalDateTime openedAt;
    private LocalDateTime closedAt;

    @Enumerated(EnumType.STRING)
    private DrawerSessionStatus status;

    private BigDecimal openingFloat;

    private BigDecimal expectedCash;

    private BigDecimal countedCash;

    private BigDecimal varianceAmount;

    private String closingNote;

    private Boolean blindClose;
}
```

Enum:

```java
public enum DrawerSessionStatus {
    OPEN,
    CLOSED,
    CANCELLED
}
```

---

## 5.4 New Entity: `CashDrawerMovement`

Tracks every financial or security event related to the drawer.

```java
public class CashDrawerMovement {
    private Long id;

    private CashDrawerSession drawerSession;

    @Enumerated(EnumType.STRING)
    private DrawerMovementType movementType;

    private BigDecimal amount;

    private String referenceType;
    private Long referenceId;

    private User performedBy;

    private CashShift employeeShift;

    private String reason;

    private LocalDateTime occurredAt;
}
```

Enum:

```java
public enum DrawerMovementType {
    OPENING_FLOAT,
    CASH_SALE,
    CASH_REFUND,
    SAFE_DROP,
    PAY_IN,
    PAY_OUT,
    NO_SALE_OPEN,
    ADJUSTMENT
}
```

Financial effect:

| Movement Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| `OPENING_FLOAT` | Sets starting cash |
| `CASH_SALE` | Increases expected drawer cash |
| `CASH_REFUND` | Decreases expected drawer cash |
| `SAFE_DROP` | Decreases expected drawer cash |
| `PAY_IN` | Increases expected drawer cash |
| `PAY_OUT` | Decreases expected drawer cash |
| `NO_SALE_OPEN` | No financial effect, audit only |
| `ADJUSTMENT` | Manager-only correction |

---

## 5.5 Update `Sale`

Add:

```java
@ManyToOne(fetch = FetchType.LAZY)
@JoinColumn(name = "employee_shift_id")
private CashShift employeeShift;
```

A sale may have non-cash payments only, so it does not always need a drawer session.

---

## 5.6 Add or Update `SalePayment`

If the system already has payment records, extend them.  
If not, create a proper split-payment table.

```java
public class SalePayment {
    private Long id;

    private Sale sale;

    @Enumerated(EnumType.STRING)
    private PaymentMethod paymentMethod;

    private BigDecimal amount;

    private String externalReference;
    // Example: wallet transaction reference, card approval code

    @Enumerated(EnumType.STRING)
    private PaymentStatus paymentStatus;

    private CashDrawerSession drawerSession;
    // Required only for CASH payments

    private CashDrawerMovement drawerMovement;
    // Linked movement for cash payment

    private LocalDateTime paidAt;
}
```

Payment methods:

```java
public enum PaymentMethod {
    CASH,
    MOBILE_WALLET,
    CARD,
    BANK_TRANSFER,
    CREDIT,
    STORE_CREDIT,
    OTHER
}
```

Payment status:

```java
public enum PaymentStatus {
    COMPLETED,
    PENDING,
    FAILED,
    REFUNDED,
    PARTIALLY_REFUNDED
}
```

# 6. Drawer Expected Cash Formula

The drawer expected cash must be calculated only from cash-related movements.

```text
Expected Cash =
Opening Float
+ Cash Sales
- Cash Refunds
- Safe Drops
+ Pay Ins
- Pay Outs
± Manager Adjustments
```

Do not include:

- Mobile wallet sales
- Card sales
- Bank transfer sales
- Credit sales
- Loyalty points
- Non-cash refunds

---

# 7. Shift Opening Requirements

## 7.1 Employee opens POS

When an employee starts working, show:

```text
Choose shift type:

[ Cash Drawer Shift ]
I will accept cash and use the drawer.

[ Non-Cash Shift ]
I will only accept wallet/card/bank/credit payments.
```

---

## 7.2 Opening a Non-Cash Shift

Backend behavior:

- Create `CashShift`
- `shiftType = NON_CASH`
- `drawerSession = null`
- `openingFloat = 0`
- No drawer validation required
- Multiple non-cash shifts can be open at the same time

Allowed:

```text
Aung - Cash Drawer Shift
Thida - Non-Cash Shift
Kyaw - Non-Cash Shift
```

---

## 7.3 Opening a Cash Drawer Shift

Backend behavior:

1. Check if a drawer session is already open.
2. If no drawer session exists:
   - Create new `CashDrawerSession`
   - Require opening float
   - Link employee shift to drawer session
3. If drawer session already exists:
   - If shared drawer mode is disabled:
     - Block opening another cash shift
   - If shared drawer mode is enabled:
     - Allow employee to join existing drawer session
     - Require manager approval if configured

---

# 8. Shared Drawer Mode

Add system setting:

```text
allowSharedCashDrawer = true / false
```

## 8.1 If disabled

Only one employee can be attached to the drawer.

Example:

```text
Aung has Main Drawer open.
Thida cannot open Cash Drawer Shift.
Thida must use Non-Cash Shift.
```

## 8.2 If enabled

Multiple employees can sell using the same drawer.

Example:

```text
Main Drawer Session #001
Opened by: Aung

Attached employees:
- Aung
- Thida
```

All cash sales still go into the same drawer session.

Reports must show:

- Total drawer cash
- Cash sales by employee
- Refunds by employee
- Variance at drawer level

Important: Variance belongs to the drawer session, not falsely to every employee.

---

# 9. Checkout Business Logic

## 9.1 Non-cash sale

If employee has `NON_CASH` shift:

Allowed:

```text
Mobile Wallet: 25,000 MMK
Card: 25,000 MMK
Bank Transfer: 25,000 MMK
Credit: 25,000 MMK
```

The system completes sale without drawer session.

---

## 9.2 Cash sale

If employee has `NON_CASH` shift and tries cash:

Block checkout.

Error message:

```text
This is a Non-Cash Shift.
Cash payments are not allowed.

Please ask the cash drawer cashier to complete this sale,
or park the sale and send it to the main counter.
```

---

## 9.3 Mixed payment sale

Example:

```text
Total: 50,000 MMK
Cash: 20,000 MMK
KBZPay: 30,000 MMK
```

This requires drawer session because part of the payment is cash.

Rules:

- If employee has active cash drawer session: allow.
- If employee is non-cash: block.
- Do not partially complete the sale.
- Do not create stock movement before payment validation succeeds.

---

## 9.4 Credit sale

Credit sale does not require drawer session.

But later, when customer pays their credit balance:

- If paid by cash: requires drawer session.
- If paid by wallet/bank/card: does not require drawer session.

---

# 10. Refund Business Logic

## 10.1 Non-cash refund

If original payment was wallet/card/bank, refund can be processed without drawer.

The system should record:

```text
refundMethod = MOBILE_WALLET / CARD / BANK_TRANSFER
employeeShiftId
refundStatus
externalReference
```

No drawer movement is created.

---

## 10.2 Cash refund

Cash refund physically removes money from drawer.

Therefore:

- Requires active cash drawer session.
- Requires permission.
- Creates `CashDrawerMovement` of type `CASH_REFUND`.
- Decreases expected drawer cash.

If a non-cash employee tries cash refund:

Block with message:

```text
Cash refund requires the cash drawer cashier or manager.
```

Optional UX:

```text
[Create Refund Request]
```

The request appears in manager/cashier inbox for approval.

---

# 11. Shift Closing Requirements

## 11.1 Closing Non-Cash Shift

Non-cash shift close should be simple.

Show summary:

```text
Non-Cash Shift Summary

Employee: Thida
Opened: 9:00 AM
Closed: 5:00 PM

Transactions: 42
Gross Sales: 620,000 MMK
Refunds: 15,000 MMK
Net Sales: 605,000 MMK

Payment Breakdown:
KBZPay: 400,000 MMK
WavePay: 180,000 MMK
Bank Transfer: 25,000 MMK

No cash drawer reconciliation required.
```

No fields for:

- Opening float
- Counted cash
- Expected cash
- Variance

---

## 11.2 Closing Cash Drawer Shift

If employee is drawer owner:

They may close the drawer session if:

- No other active cash employees are attached, or
- Manager approves transfer/closure

Show blind cash close screen:

```text
Count the drawer physically.
Do not show expected amount before count is submitted.
```

Fields:

- Actual counted cash
- Closing note
- Manager approval if variance exceeds tolerance

After submit:

- Calculate expected cash
- Calculate variance
- Close drawer session
- Close employee shift

---

## 11.3 Closing one employee shift should not always close the drawer

If multiple employees are attached to one drawer:

- Thida closing her shift should not close Aung’s drawer.
- Drawer closes only when drawer owner/manager closes the drawer session.

---

# 12. Drawer Operations

Only cash-authorized users can access drawer operations.

## 12.1 Safe Drop

Used when too much cash is in the drawer.

Example:

```text
Drawer has 1,500,000 MMK.
Manager removes 1,000,000 MMK to safe.
```

System records:

```text
SAFE_DROP -1,000,000 MMK
```

Expected drawer cash decreases.

---

## 12.2 Pay In

Money added to drawer outside normal sale.

Example:

```text
Owner adds small change coins/notes.
```

Creates positive drawer movement.

---

## 12.3 Pay Out

Money removed from drawer for shop expense.

Example:

```text
Paid delivery driver from drawer.
```

Creates negative drawer movement.

Requires reason and permission.

---

## 12.4 No-Sale Drawer Open

If drawer is opened without sale:

Record:

```text
NO_SALE_OPEN
amount = 0
performedBy
employeeShiftId
reason
timestamp
```

Show in manager report.

---

# 15. API Requirements

## 15.1 Open employee shift

```http
POST /api/shifts/open
```

Request:

```json
{
  "shiftType": "NON_CASH"
}
```

or

```json
{
  "shiftType": "CASH_DRAWER",
  "drawerId": 1,
  "openingFloat": 100000
}
```

Response:

```json
{
  "shiftId": 123,
  "shiftType": "NON_CASH",
  "drawerSessionId": null,
  "cashAllowed": false,
  "status": "OPEN"
}
```

---

## 15.2 Get current shift

```http

```

Response:

```json

  "shiftId": 123,
```

Before checkout, backend must validate payment methods.

```http
POST /api/sales/validate-payment
```

Request:

```json

      "method": "CASH",
      "amount": 20000

      "method": "MOBILE_WALLET",
```

# 17. Migration Requirements

## 17.1 Existing data

Existing cash shifts should remain valid.

Migration strategy:

1. Create default `CashDrawer` named `Main Drawer`.
2. Existing historical `CashShift` rows default to `CASH_DRAWER`.
3. For currently open old cash shifts:
   - Create a `CashDrawerSession`
   - Use old opening float
   - Link old shift to drawer session
4. Existing closed shifts do not need recalculation.
5. Existing sale records should not have financial values changed.

---

## 17.2 Backward compatibility

Existing reports must continue to work.

If old sales do not have `employeeShiftId`, allow null for historical records.

New sales must require employee shift.

# 18. Acceptance Criteria

## 18.1 Non-cash employee can sell without drawer

Given Thida has a `NON_CASH` shift,  
when she completes a sale using `MOBILE_WALLET`,  
then the sale succeeds and no drawer session is required.

---

## 18.2 Non-cash employee cannot accept cash

Given Thida has a `NON_CASH` shift,  
when she tries to checkout with `CASH`,  
then the backend rejects the sale with `CASH_NOT_ALLOWED_FOR_SHIFT`.

---

## 18.3 Only one drawer session can be open

Given Main Drawer already has an open session,  
when another user tries to open a new drawer session for Main Drawer,  
then the system blocks it unless joining existing drawer is allowed.

---

## 18.4 Mixed payment requires drawer

Given sale total is 50,000,  
and payment is 20,000 cash + 30,000 wallet,  
when a non-cash employee tries to checkout,  
then checkout is blocked.

---

## 18.5 Non-cash shift close has no variance

Given Thida has only wallet sales,  
when she closes her shift,  
then the system shows digital payment summary only and does not ask for cash count.

---

## 18.6 Drawer report includes all cash activity

Given Aung and Thida both sold cash under the same drawer session,  
when drawer is closed,  
then expected cash includes both employees’ cash sales.

---

## 18.7 Cash refund reduces drawer expected cash

Given a cash refund of 10,000 is processed,  
when drawer expected cash is calculated,  
then expected cash decreases by 10,000.

---

## 18.8 Wallet refund does not affect drawer

Given a wallet refund of 10,000 is processed,  
when drawer expected cash is calculated,  
then drawer expected cash is unchanged.

---

# 19. Out of Scope

Do not implement:

- Money rounding
- Cash rounding
- Multi-branch cloud sync
- Online SaaS subscription logic
- Remote branch drawer management

This feature is only for improving real-world shop shift behavior in offline/LAN usage.

---

# 20. Summary for AI Coding Agent

Implement a separation between **employee work shifts** and **physical cash drawer sessions**.

The most important rule:

```text
Non-cash sales require an employee shift.
Cash sales require both an employee shift and an active cash drawer session.
```

Build the system so:

- Wallet-only employees can work without touching the drawer.
- One physical drawer has one reconciled cash session.
- Multiple employees can sell without creating fake cash variances.
- Cash variance is calculated only for the real drawer.
- Employee accountability is preserved for every sale.
- End-of-day reports clearly separate cash drawer money from digital revenue.
